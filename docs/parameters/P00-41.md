## In plain words

The drive has two supplies. The main power drives the motor. The control power runs the electronics of the drive. This protection acts when the control power fails.

## How it works

- **0**: off.
- **1**: on. The drive detects a loss of the control power and stops in a controlled way.

## On your machine

Keep the default value 1. A drive that loses its control power without this protection can stop in an uncontrolled way.
