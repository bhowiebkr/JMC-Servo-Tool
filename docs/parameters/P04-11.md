## In plain words

The front panel of the drive can show that the motor turns. This parameter sets the speed above which the drive counts the motor as turning.

## How it works

The unit is rpm. The factory value is 30 rpm. Above this speed, the front panel shows that the motor turns.

## On your machine

This setting changes only the display. It has no effect on the movement.
