## In plain words

A multi-turn absolute encoder knows two things: how many turns the motor made, and the angle in the current turn. A Modbus master can read these two numbers from two registers. This parameter selects which register holds which number.

## How it works

| Value | Register 0x84D | Register 0x84E |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Turns | Position in the turn |
| 1 | Position in the turn | Turns |

The motor position is the turns multiplied by the counts in one turn, plus the position in the turn.

## On your machine

This setting is only for a controller that reads the absolute position over Modbus. LinuxCNC with step and direction does not use it.
