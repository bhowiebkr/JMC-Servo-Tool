## In plain words

On an RS485 line, only one device can talk at a time. After a master sends a question, it must change from talking to listening. Some masters need a moment for this. This delay makes the drive wait before it answers.

## How it works

- **0**: the drive answers at the normal time.
- **Other values**: the drive waits this many milliseconds before it answers.

If the drive answers too soon, the master misses the start of the answer. The master then reports a timeout or a bad message.

## On your machine

This tool does not write this parameter. Most USB to RS485 adapters change direction fast, so 0 works. Increase it from the front panel only if the link gives many timeouts.
