## In plain words

The rated speed is the speed that the motor can keep all day at its rated torque. It is like the cruising speed of a car, not its top speed.

## How it works

Many servo motors have a rated speed of 3000 rpm and a maximum speed of 5000 or 6000 rpm. Above the rated speed, the motor gives less torque, because its voltage gets close to the supply voltage.

The motor power is the rated torque (P00-02) multiplied by the rated speed. For example, 1.27 N·m at 3000 rpm is approximately 400 W.

## On your machine

This value comes from the motor. With the motor code 2000 (P00-00), the drive reads it from the encoder. Use it to plan your axis: with a 5 mm screw, 3000 rpm gives 15 000 mm per minute.

Do not change it. The speed limits that you can set are P04-06 and P04-07.
