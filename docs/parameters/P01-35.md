## In plain words

The drive can send the encoder position to another controller on its encoder output. The Z pulse on this output marks one point in each motor turn. Some controllers read inputs too slowly to see a short pulse. This parameter makes the Z pulse longer.

## How it works

- **0**: the Z pulse has its normal width. The width depends on the speed.
- **Other values**: the Z pulse lasts this many milliseconds.

A PLC input often needs a pulse of a few milliseconds. A fast counter card sees a short pulse.

## On your machine

LinuxCNC with a step and direction card does not use the encoder output. If you use it for homing to the Z pulse, a fast encoder input sees the normal pulse. Keep 0 then.
