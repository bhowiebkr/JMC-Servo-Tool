## In plain words

An absolute encoder sends its position as a number on a serial line. The drive and the encoder must speak the same language. This parameter selects the language.

## How it works

Tamagawa and Nikon are two encoder makers. Each has its own serial protocol. Many other encoder makers use one of these two protocols.

This setting applies only to absolute encoders (P00-07 = 2 or 3).

## On your machine

This value comes from the motor. Do not change it. A wrong value gives an encoder communication alarm, and the motor does not run.
