## In plain words

The home switch is seldom exactly where you want the zero of the axis. The home offset is the distance from the switch to the real zero. The drive stores it in two parts, because the value can be larger than one parameter holds. This is the high part.

## How it works

P03-70 is the low part. The manual does not say how the drive combines the two parts, or which unit it uses.

## On your machine

This applies only to the homing function in the drive. LinuxCNC has its own home offset in its configuration file.
