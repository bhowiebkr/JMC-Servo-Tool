## In plain words

A controller can send movement commands as pulses in three different "languages". The drive must listen in the same language that the controller speaks. Otherwise the motor moves wrongly or not at all.

## How it works

| Value | Format | How the pulses work |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | A/B quadrature | Two square waves. The order of their edges gives the direction. |
| 1 | Pulse and direction | One wire gives a pulse for each step. A second wire is high or low for the direction. |
| 2 or 3 | CW and CCW | One wire gives pulses for forward. A second wire gives pulses for reverse. |

Pulse and direction is the most common format. Most stepper and servo controllers use it.

## On your machine

LinuxCNC with a step generator normally uses pulse and direction. Keep the factory value 1. If you change the step type in LinuxCNC, change this value to match. Change it only with the servo off.
