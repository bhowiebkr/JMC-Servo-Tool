## In plain words

The rated current is the current that the motor can take all the time without getting too hot. In an electric motor, torque comes from current. More current gives more torque.

## How it works

The drive uses this value to protect the motor. It compares the real current with the rated current over time. If the motor takes too much current for too long, the drive stops with an overload alarm. This protection works like a thermal fuse that never needs a replacement.

## On your machine

This value comes from the motor. Do not change it. A value that is too high lets the motor overheat. A value that is too low gives false alarms and less torque.
