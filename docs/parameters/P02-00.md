## In plain words

Imagine that the motor is tied to its target position with a spring. When the motor falls behind, the spring pulls it forward. This gain is the strength of that spring. A strong spring pulls hard even at a small error, so the motor stays close to the target. A spring that is too strong makes the motor overshoot and bounce.

## How it works

The position loop gain sets how strongly the drive corrects the following error. The following error is the difference between the commanded position and the motor position.

The drive multiplies the following error by this gain. The result is the speed command for the speed loop. The unit is 1/s. At a gain of 48.0, a following error of 1 turn gives a speed command of 48 turns per second.

## What a change does

- A higher gain gives a smaller following error and a faster stop at the target.
- A gain that is too high for the speed loop causes overshoot and vibration at the end of a move.
- A lower gain gives a softer response and a larger following error.

At a constant speed, the following error is the speed divided by the gain. Speed feedforward (P02-03) makes this error smaller.

## How to set it

Set the auto-tuning mode (P01-02) to 0 first. In modes 1 to 3, the drive sets this gain itself, and a value that you write has no effect.

1. Set the speed loop first: P02-10 and P02-11.
2. Increase this gain in small steps. Look at the following error on the Live tab during a move.
3. Stop when the motor overshoots or makes noise at the end of a move. Then decrease the gain by 20 %.

A common rule: the position loop gain is between one fifth and one third of 2π × P02-10. For P02-10 = 27.0 Hz, this is 34 to 57 1/s.

## Gain set 2

P02-01 is the position loop gain of gain set 2. The drive uses gain set 2 when gain switching (P02-30) selects it.

## On your machine

On a CNC machine, a small following error keeps the tool on the path. Corners stay sharp, and circles stay round. With the factory auto-tuning mode 1 (P01-02), the drive sets this gain from the stiffness level. So change the stiffness level (P01-03) in place of this gain.
