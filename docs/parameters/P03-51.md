## In plain words

The drive reads the position of the other gantry motor. The two motors often face in opposite directions on the two sides of the gantry. This parameter turns the direction of the other motor's signal around, so both count the same way.

## How it works

- **0 Normal**
- **1 Inverted**

If the direction is wrong, the drive sees a growing difference when both sides move together. It then trips AL.510 quickly.

## On your machine

This matters only with gantry synchronization on (P03-50 = 1). Change it only with the servo off.
