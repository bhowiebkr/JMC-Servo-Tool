## In plain words

In speed mode, a controller can set the motor speed with a voltage from −10 V to +10 V. More voltage gives more speed, and a negative voltage turns the motor in reverse. This parameter sets how many rpm each volt gives.

## How it works

The unit is rpm for each volt. The factory value is 300, so 10 V gives 3000 rpm.

To find the value, divide the top speed that you need by 10 V.

## Example

A spindle motor must reach 3000 rpm at 10 V. 3000 ÷ 10 = 300, which is the factory value. For 2000 rpm at 10 V, set 200.

## On your machine

A CNC axis runs in position mode and does not use the analog input. A spindle that LinuxCNC controls with a 0 to 10 V signal uses it.
