## In plain words

Between two steps of the screwdriver sequence, the drive can pause for a moment. The pause lets the screw and the material settle before the next step.

## How it works

The unit is ms. The factory value is 100 ms.

## On your machine

The screwdriver sequence is for a screwdriving machine, not for a CNC axis.
