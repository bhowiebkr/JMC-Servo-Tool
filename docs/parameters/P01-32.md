## In plain words

At servo off with the motor turning, the drive waits for the motor to slow down before it closes the holding brake. This parameter is the longest time that it waits.

## How it works

The drive closes the brake at the first of these two events:

- The speed falls below P01-31.
- This time ends.

A short time protects a vertical axis, because the axis cannot fall far. A long time protects the brake, because it closes at a lower speed.

## On your machine

The factory value of 50 ms is a good balance for most machines. On a heavy vertical axis, keep it short.
