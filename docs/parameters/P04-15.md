## In plain words

This parameter sets how fast the motor slows down in speed mode. A gentle deceleration is easier on the machine. It also sends less braking energy back into the drive.

## How it works

The unit is milliseconds for each 1000 rpm. 0 gives no ramp: the motor decelerates as fast as it can.

A fast stop of a heavy load sends a lot of energy back into the drive. If the braking resistor cannot take it, the drive stops with an overvoltage alarm. A longer ramp helps then.

## On your machine

This applies in speed mode only. In position mode, LinuxCNC plans the deceleration with its own settings.
