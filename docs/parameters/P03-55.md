## In plain words

When the two gantry motors drift apart, the drive pushes them back together. This gain sets how hard it pushes.

## How it works

The correction is proportional to the difference. A higher gain removes the difference faster. A gain that is too high can make the two sides fight each other and vibrate. The factory value is 10.

## On your machine

This matters only with gantry synchronization on (P03-50 = 1). Increase it in small steps while you watch the difference.
