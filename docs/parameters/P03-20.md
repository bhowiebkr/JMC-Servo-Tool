## In plain words

Normally the drive measures the position at the motor, with the motor encoder. But the screw, the belt and the nut between the motor and the table can stretch or have play. A linear scale measures the real position of the table. This parameter lets the drive use that scale.

## How it works

- **0 Motor encoder**: the position loop uses the encoder in the motor. This is the factory setting.
- **1 Linear scale**: the position loop uses a scale on the axis. This is called full closed loop control.

With a scale, the drive corrects errors of the screw and the belt. If the scale and the motor disagree too much, the drive stops with AL.502.

## On your machine

Most CNC machines use the motor encoder. A scale must connect to the drive, and P03-30 and P03-31 must match its signals. Test full closed loop control carefully at low speed first.
