## In plain words

The braking circuit can fail in two ways. The resistor can be open, for example from a broken wire. Or it can be shorted, for example from a damaged cable. This check finds both faults.

## How it works

- **0**: no check.
- **1**: the drive checks the braking circuit for an open circuit and a short circuit. It stops with the alarm AL.432 if it finds a fault.

An open resistor is dangerous, because the drive cannot get rid of braking energy. Then the voltage can rise high enough to damage the drive.

## On your machine

The check is a good idea with an external resistor, because its wires can break or come loose.
