## In plain words

In position mode, the drive needs someone to tell it where to go. This parameter selects who that is: a stream of pulses on the wires, or a number that a controller sends over a data link.

## How it works

- **0 Pulse input**: each pulse on the pulse input moves the motor one small step. The motor follows the pulses as they arrive. This is the factory setting.
- **1 Digital**: a controller sends a target position as a number over a field bus. The drive then plans the move itself. P03-45 and P03-46 control these moves.

## On your machine

LinuxCNC sends step and direction pulses, so keep 0. Change it only with the servo off.
