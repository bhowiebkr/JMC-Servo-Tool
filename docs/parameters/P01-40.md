## In plain words

A motor can run out of control if something is badly wrong. Examples are swapped motor wires, a wrong encoder or a damaged encoder cable. Then the motor can turn fast in the wrong direction. Runaway detection sees this and stops the motor with an alarm.

## How it works

The drive compares the direction and the speed of the motor with the command. If the motor turns the wrong way or accelerates without a command, the drive stops it. The alarm is AL.422.

- **0**: off.
- **1**: on. This is the factory setting.

## On your machine

Keep it on. A runaway axis can crash into its end at full speed. If you see AL.422 at the first start, check the order of the motor wires U, V and W, and check the encoder cable.
