## In plain words

When the drive finds a fault, it stops driving the motor. A turning motor then coasts. This parameter selects if the drive uses the dynamic brake to stop the motor faster after an alarm.

## How it works

- **0**: use the dynamic brake at an alarm.
- **1**: do not use it. The motor coasts. This is the factory setting.

The dynamic brake connects the motor windings together, so the turning motor brakes itself. It does not hold the motor at a stop.

## On your machine

An alarm during a fast move, for example a following error after a crash, lets the axis coast. A dynamic brake shortens this distance. This can reduce damage to the tool and the work.
