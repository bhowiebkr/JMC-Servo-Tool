## In plain words

To make torque, the drive must put the current into the coils at the correct angle to the rotor magnets. This is called commutation. The Z pulse gives the drive an exact reference point for this angle.

## How it works

An incremental encoder gives one Z pulse in each turn. The motor maker measures the angle of the rotor magnets at this pulse and stores it in this parameter. When the drive sees the first Z pulse, it knows the exact magnet angle from then on.

Before the first Z pulse, the drive uses a rough angle from the start angles (P00-12 to P00-17).

## On your machine

This value comes from the motor and applies only to incremental encoders. Do not change it. A wrong value gives less torque, more heat, and in a bad case a motor that runs out of control.
