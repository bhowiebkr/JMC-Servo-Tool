## In plain words

At the end of the screwdriver sequence, the motor can turn back a little. This takes the tension off the bit, so it comes out of the screw head easily. This parameter is the speed of that back-off.

## How it works

The unit is rpm. P05-25 sets how far the motor turns back, and P05-28 sets the torque limit. The factory value is 0.

## On your machine

The screwdriver sequence is for a screwdriving machine, not for a CNC axis.
