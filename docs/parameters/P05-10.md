## In plain words

This is the most a motor can push in the forward direction. It works like a slipping clutch. In a crash, the motor pushes no harder than this limit, so the force on the machine stays lower.

## How it works

The unit is percent of the rated torque (P00-02). The factory value is 200 %.

This limit applies all the time, in every mode. A digital input with function 7 changes to the lower limit P05-12. When the motor is at the limit, the drive sets the torque limit output.

## The balance

A low limit has a cost. During a fast move, the motor needs a high torque to accelerate. If the limit is too low, the motor cannot follow. The following error grows, and the drive stops with AL.501.

So set the limit above the torque that your fastest moves need. The Live tab shows the torque during a move.

## On your machine

The torque limit and the following error alarm (P03-15) work together. The limit keeps the force low in a crash. The alarm stops the machine when the axis cannot follow. Set both, and test them with gentle moves.
