## In plain words

To measure the load inertia, the drive moves the motor back and forth. It measures how much torque each change of speed needs. A heavy load needs more torque for the same change. This parameter sets the top speed of those test moves.

## How it works

The unit is rpm. The range is 100 to 2000 rpm, and the factory value is 800 rpm.

A higher speed gives a stronger signal and a better measurement. But the motor also turns further in each move (P08-05).

## How to measure

1. Make sure that the axis can move freely in both directions.
2. On the front panel, press M and select AF_J-L. Then press ENT.
3. Hold the up key or the down key. The motor moves back and forth until the ratio appears.
4. Press M to exit. Write the result into P01-04.

## On your machine

Before you start, move the axis to the middle of its travel. Check the travel in P08-05, so that the axis cannot hit an end stop. If the measurement fails, the drive stops with AL.550.
