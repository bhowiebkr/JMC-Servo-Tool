## In plain words

This is the torque limit while the motor turns back at the end of the screwdriver sequence. The back-off must be strong enough to free the bit, but not so strong that it loosens the screw.

## How it works

The unit is percent of the rated torque. The factory value is 200 %.

## On your machine

The screwdriver sequence is for a screwdriving machine, not for a CNC axis.
