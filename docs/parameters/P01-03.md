## What it does

The drive has 32 built-in gain sets, from level 0 to level 31. This parameter selects one of them. It applies only when the auto-tuning mode (P01-02) is 1, 2 or 3.

A higher level gives higher position and speed loop gains. The motor holds its position more stiffly and follows the command more closely.

## Effect

- A higher level gives a smaller following error and a faster response.
- Too high a level for the machine causes noise, vibration or resonance.
- A lower level gives a softer and quieter response.

The right level depends on the load inertia ratio (P01-04). Set the ratio correctly first.

## How to set it

1. Start at the factory level, 13.
2. Increase the level by one step. Run a move and listen to the motor. Look at the following error on the Live tab.
3. Continue until the motor makes noise or vibrates at a stop. Then go back two levels.
4. On a machine with several axes, give the axes that move together a similar following error. Different following errors change the shape of curves and corners.
