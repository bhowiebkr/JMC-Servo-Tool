## In plain words

One parameter can hold a number up to 65535. Some gear ratios need a larger top number. This parameter extends the numerator of electronic gear 1.

## How it works

The drive builds the numerator from two parts:

numerator = P03-12 × 10000 + P03-10

Note that the split is at 10000, not at 65536.

## Example

You need a numerator of 65536. That is too large for P03-10 alone. Set this parameter to 6 and P03-10 to 5536. Then 6 × 10000 + 5536 = 65536.

## On your machine

Most machines do not need this. Keep 0 unless your gear ratio needs a numerator above 65535. A change applies after a power cycle. Change it only with the servo off.
