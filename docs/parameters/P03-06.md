## In plain words

No motor stops at exactly the target. It stops within a very small distance of it. This parameter sets how close is close enough for the drive to say "I am in position".

## How it works

The unit is encoder counts, not command pulses.

- An absolute encoder gives 131072 counts in each turn.
- An incremental encoder gives 4 counts for each line (see P00-10).

## Example

With an absolute encoder, the factory value 100 is 100 ÷ 131072 of one turn. That is less than 0.3° of the motor shaft. On a 5 mm ball screw, it is less than 0.004 mm.

## On your machine

The factory value is fine for most machines. A smaller value gives a more exact signal, but the output comes on later. If the value is too small, the output may flicker while the motor holds its position.
