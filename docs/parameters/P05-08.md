## In plain words

The screwdriver sequence tightens a screw in up to four steps, each with its own torque limit. This parameter selects the unit of those torque limits.

## How it works

| Value | Unit of the torque limits |
|---|---|
| 0 | Percent of the rated torque. This is the factory setting. |
| 1 | 0.01 N·m |
| 2 | Step 4 in 0.01 N·m, the other steps in percent |

A unit in N·m is useful when the screw has a specified torque. Then the value does not depend on the motor.

## Example

A screw needs 1.5 N·m. With unit 1, set 150.

## On your machine

The screwdriver sequence is for a screwdriving machine, not for a CNC axis. The manual links it to control mode 6 (see P01-01).
