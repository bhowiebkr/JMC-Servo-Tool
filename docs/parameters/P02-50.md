## In plain words

A vertical axis, such as a Z axis, always pulls down because of gravity. The motor must push up all the time just to hold it still. This parameter gives the drive this holding torque from the start, so the position loop does not have to find it from an error.

## How it works

The drive adds this fixed torque to the torque command. It applies in position mode only. The unit is percent of the rated torque (P00-02).

Without an offset, the integral part of the speed loop finds the holding torque. But it needs a small position error to do so. After each move, the axis can sag a little before the integral catches up.

## How to set it

1. Hold the vertical axis still with the servo on.
2. Read the torque command. Front panel monitor item 9 shows it (see P00-20).
3. Set this value to the torque that you read. The sign depends on the direction of gravity for your motor.

## On your machine

A Z axis with a counterweight or a gas spring needs little or no offset. A horizontal axis needs no offset. Leave it at 0 then.
