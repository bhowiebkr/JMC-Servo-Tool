## In plain words

A notch filter can remove its frequency fully, or only reduce it. This parameter sets how deep the notch is.

## How it works

The range is 0 to 99. The value is how much of the center frequency gets through, in percent. 0 removes the center frequency fully, and this is the factory value.

- A deeper notch (a lower value) removes more of the resonance.
- A shallower notch (a higher value) adds less delay.

## On your machine

Start at 0. If the vibration stays away with margin, increase the value a little to reduce the delay.
