## In plain words

A linear scale sends two signals, A and B. The order of their edges tells the direction of movement. If the scale is mounted the other way round, it reports the wrong direction. This parameter turns the direction of the scale around.

## How it works

- **0 Normal**
- **1 Reversed**: the drive swaps the order of A and B.

If the motor and the scale count in opposite directions, full closed loop control pushes the axis the wrong way.

## On your machine

This matters only if a linear scale connects to the drive (P03-20 = 1). Check the direction with the servo off: move the axis by hand and watch that the motor and the scale count the same way. Change it only with the servo off.
