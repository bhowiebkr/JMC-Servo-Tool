## In plain words

A holding brake is made to hold a still axis, not to stop a moving one. If the servo goes off while the motor still turns, the drive waits for the motor to slow down before it closes the brake. This parameter is the speed that counts as slow enough.

## How it works

At servo off with the motor turning:

1. The drive takes the current away from the motor, and the motor slows down.
2. When the speed falls below this value, the drive closes the brake.
3. If the speed does not fall below this value within the time P01-32, the drive closes the brake anyway.

## On your machine

The factory value of 100 rpm suits most motors. A lower value saves the brake, but a vertical axis drops further before the brake closes.
