## In plain words

This value shows how far the motor turns in each test move of the inertia measurement. You cannot set it. The drive calculates it from the speed P08-02, the ramp P08-03 and the wait P08-04.

## How it works

The unit is motor turns. The factory settings give 1.33 turns.

## Example

At 800 rpm, the motor makes 13.3 turns each second. A ramp of 100 ms up to this speed takes 0.67 turns. The ramp down takes another 0.67 turns. So one test move is 1.33 turns.

## On your machine

Convert the turns to axis travel. With a ball screw that moves 5 mm for each turn, 1.33 turns move the axis 6.7 mm. Make sure that the axis has at least this much free space in both directions.
