## In plain words

Inertia is how hard it is to start or stop a rotation. A heavy flywheel has a high inertia. A small fan has a low inertia. The rotor inertia is the inertia of the rotating part in the motor.

## How it works

The drive compares the inertia of your machine with this value. The load inertia ratio (P01-04) is the load inertia divided by this rotor inertia. The drive uses the ratio to know how much torque it needs to accelerate the axis.

A low-inertia motor reacts fast. A motor with more inertia is calmer with a heavy load. A load inertia below approximately 10 times the rotor inertia is easy to tune.

## On your machine

This value comes from the motor. Do not change it. A wrong value makes the inertia ratio wrong, and then the gains do not give the response that you expect.
