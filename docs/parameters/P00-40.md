## In plain words

Electronic parts get hot when they work hard. Too much heat damages them. The drive has a temperature sensor in its power module, and it stops before the heat causes damage.

## How it works

- **0**: no temperature protection. Do not use this.
- **1**: the drive uses its internal sensor.
- **2**: the drive uses an external sensor.
- **3**: the drive finds the sensor automatically.

When the temperature is too high, the drive stops with the alarm AL.440.

## On your machine

Keep the default value 1. If the drive gets hot, give it more space and air in the cabinet. A cabinet fan helps. Monitor item 24 on the front panel shows the drive temperature (see P00-20).
