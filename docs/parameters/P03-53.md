## In plain words

If one side of a gantry jams or slips, the two motors drift apart. This parameter is the largest difference that the drive allows. A larger difference stops the drive with an alarm, before the gantry twists too far.

## How it works

The drive compares the positions of the two gantry motors. If the difference is larger than this value, it stops with AL.510. The manual does not give the unit. It is probably feedback pulses.

## On your machine

This matters only with gantry synchronization on (P03-50 = 1). Set it small enough to protect the gantry, but larger than the normal difference during fast moves.
