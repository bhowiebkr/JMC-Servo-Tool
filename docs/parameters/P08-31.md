## In plain words

A notch filter removes a band of frequencies around its center frequency (P08-30). This parameter sets how wide that band is.

## How it works

This is the width of notch 1, relative to its center frequency (P08-30). The range is 0 to 20. A higher value gives a wider notch.

## What a change does

- A wider notch removes a resonance that moves a little, for example with the axis position.
- A wider notch also adds more delay below the center frequency.
- A narrow notch adds little delay, but it must be exactly on the resonance.

## How to set it

Start at the factory value, 2. Use the narrowest notch that stops the vibration at all axis positions.
