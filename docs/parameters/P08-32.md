## What it does

This is the depth of notch 1. It is the output divided by the input at the center frequency (P08-30), in %. The range is 0 to 99. 0 gives the deepest notch, which removes the center frequency fully.

## Effect

- A deeper notch (a lower value) removes more of the resonance.
- A shallower notch (a higher value) adds less delay.

## How to set it

Start at 0. If the vibration stays away with margin, increase the value a little to reduce the delay.
