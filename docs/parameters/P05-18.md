## In plain words

The screwdriver sequence tightens a screw in up to four steps. Each step has its own speed and its own torque limit. This is the speed of step 1. Step 1 normally drives the screw in fast, with a low torque.

## How it works

The unit is rpm. The torque limit of this step is P05-19. The drive data does not say exactly when a step ends. In a typical screwdriver, a step ends when the torque reaches its limit. Then the next step starts after the wait P05-17.

## On your machine

The screwdriver sequence is for a screwdriving machine, not for a CNC axis.
