## In plain words

A braking resistor can turn only a certain amount of energy into heat each second. This is its power rating, in watts. The drive uses this value to know when the resistor is about to overheat.

## How it works

The unit is 10 W. For example, 4 means 40 W, and 10 means 100 W. Use the continuous power from the label of the resistor, not its short-time power.

The drive estimates the heat in the resistor. If the average braking power goes above this rating, the drive stops with the alarm AL.431 before the resistor burns.

## On your machine

This value applies only with an external resistor (P00-30 = 1). Set it from the label of the resistor that you mount.
