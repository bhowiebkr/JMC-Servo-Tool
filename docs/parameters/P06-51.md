## In plain words

When a screw is tight, the drive can tell the controller with a complete output. This parameter selects when the drive sets that output.

## How it works

- **0**: after all the steps and the back-off.
- **1**: after step 3, and again after the back-off.

The manual does not name the output function for it.

## On your machine

The screwdriver sequence is for a screwdriving machine, not for a CNC axis.
