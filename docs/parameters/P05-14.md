## In plain words

When the screwdriver sequence ends, the motor can let go of the screw, or it can keep holding it. This parameter selects which.

## How it works

- **0 Motor free**: the motor has no current. This is the factory setting.
- **1 Motor holds**: the motor holds its position.
- **2**: reserved.

## On your machine

The screwdriver sequence is for a screwdriving machine, not for a CNC axis.
