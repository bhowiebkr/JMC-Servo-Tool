## In plain words

The home offset is the distance from the home switch to the real zero of the axis. This is the low part of the offset. P03-69 is the high part.

## How it works

The factory value is 1000. The manual does not say how the drive combines the two parts, or which unit it uses.

## On your machine

This applies only to the homing function in the drive. LinuxCNC has its own home offset in its configuration file.
