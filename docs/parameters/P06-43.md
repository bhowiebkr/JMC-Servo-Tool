## In plain words

In torque mode, a controller can set the motor torque with a voltage from −10 V to +10 V. More voltage gives more torque. This parameter sets how much torque each volt gives.

## How it works

The unit is percent of the rated torque for each volt. The factory value is 10, so 10 V gives 100 % of the rated torque.

## Example

With 30, 10 V gives 300 % of the rated torque. 5 V then gives 150 %.

## On your machine

A CNC axis runs in position mode and does not use the analog input.
