## In plain words

A wide gantry often has two motors, one on each side. If one side moves more than the other, the gantry twists and can jam. Gantry synchronization lets the drive watch the other motor and keep both sides together.

## How it works

The drive reads the position of the other gantry motor as pulses. It compares this position with its own. Then it corrects the difference with the gain P03-55. If the difference becomes too large, the drive stops with AL.510.

Two digital input functions belong to this feature. Function 19 turns the synchronization on, and function 20 clears the difference.

- **0 Off**: this is the factory setting.
- **1 On**

The manual gives few details about the wiring.

## On your machine

LinuxCNC can drive a gantry with two joints on one axis. Each side then has its own drive and its own home switch. LinuxCNC squares the gantry when it homes. In that case, keep this function off. Change it only with the servo off.
