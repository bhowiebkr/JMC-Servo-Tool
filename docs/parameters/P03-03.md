## In plain words

If the axis moves the wrong way, you can turn the direction around in software. You do not have to change any wires.

## How it works

- **0 Normal**: a forward command turns the motor in its normal forward direction.
- **1 Reversed**: the drive counts each pulse in the opposite direction.

## On your machine

LinuxCNC can also reverse an axis with a negative scale in its configuration file. Use one of the two ways, not both. Otherwise the two reversals cancel each other.

Change it only with the servo off. After a change, check the direction with a slow jog before you run a program.
