## In plain words

The screwdriver sequence can tighten a screw or loosen it. This parameter selects which.

## How it works

- **0 Tighten**: the drive runs the steps of the sequence.
- **1 Loosen**: the motor turns the screw out. The speed is P05-02, and the torque limit is P05-10.

## On your machine

The screwdriver sequence is for a screwdriving machine, not for a CNC axis.
