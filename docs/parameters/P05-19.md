## In plain words

The screwdriver sequence tightens a screw in up to four steps. Each step has its own speed and its own torque limit. This is the torque limit of step 1. Step 1 normally drives the screw in fast, with a low torque.

## How it works

The unit is percent of the rated torque, or 0.01 N·m, as P05-08 selects. 0 means that the drive skips this step. The speed of this step is P05-18.

## On your machine

The screwdriver sequence is for a screwdriving machine, not for a CNC axis.
