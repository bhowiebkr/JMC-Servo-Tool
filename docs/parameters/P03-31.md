## In plain words

A linear scale gives a reference mark, the Z signal, at one point along its length. A signal can mean "active" when it is high, or when it is low. This parameter tells the drive which level of the Z signal is the active one.

## How it works

- **0 Active low**
- **1 Active high**: this is the factory setting.

The data sheet of the scale gives the correct level.

## On your machine

This matters only if a linear scale connects to the drive. Change it only with the servo off.
