## In plain words

The rated torque is the twisting force that the motor can give all the time without getting too hot. It is the 100 % mark for every torque setting in the drive.

## How it works

Torque values in the drive are in percent of this value. A torque limit of 200 % means twice the rated torque.

For a short time, a servo motor can give much more than its rated torque. Many motors give up to 300 % for a fraction of a second. This extra torque lets the axis accelerate fast. If the high torque stays too long, the motor gets hot, and the drive stops with an overload alarm.

## Example

A 400 W motor at 3000 rpm has a rated torque of approximately 1.27 N·m. A torque limit of 300 % then allows approximately 3.8 N·m.

## On your machine

This value comes from the motor. Do not change it, because a wrong value changes the meaning of every torque limit.
