## In plain words

A holding brake is a mechanical brake inside the motor. A spring closes it, and current opens it. It needs a short time to open and to close. This parameter gives the brake that time, so that the axis never drops and the motor never fights a closed brake.

## How it works

- **At servo on**: the drive energizes the motor and opens the brake. It waits this time before it accepts commands. So the motor does not try to turn against a brake that is still closed.
- **At servo off, motor stopped**: the drive closes the brake. It keeps the motor energized for this time. So the motor holds the axis until the brake really grips.

The drive controls the brake through a digital output with function 2. By default, this is DO2 (P06-23).

## On your machine

This matters for an axis with a holding brake, normally a vertical Z axis. If the Z axis drops a little at servo off, increase this time. A typical brake needs 20 to 100 ms. The data sheet of the motor gives its brake times.
