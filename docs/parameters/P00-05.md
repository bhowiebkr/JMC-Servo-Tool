## In plain words

A servo motor has permanent magnets on its rotor. The pole pairs are the number of north and south magnet pairs around the rotor. The drive must know this number to switch the current in the coils at the correct time.

## How it works

The drive turns the magnetic field in the coils, and the magnets follow it. One turn of the field moves the rotor by one pole pair. So a motor with 4 pole pairs needs 4 turns of the field for one turn of the shaft.

For example, at 3000 rpm a motor with 4 pole pairs needs an electrical frequency of 3000 ÷ 60 × 4 = 200 Hz.

## On your machine

This value comes from the motor. Do not change it. A wrong value makes the motor jerk, run at the wrong speed or not turn at all.
